## Chosen Strategy: Open-Core with Compliance Vertical

### Rationale

VECTRA's value proposition is **trust, not speed**.

| Dimension | VECTRA Strength |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Determinism | ✅ Guaranteed |
| Losslessness | ✅ Verified |
| Integrity | ✅ Cryptographic |
| Performance | ⚠️ Research-grade |

This positions VECTRA for **compliance-critical markets**, not general-purpose compression.

---

## Target Market

### Primary: Regulatory Compliance Archives

| Industry | Use Case | Pain Point |
|----------|----------|------------|
| Financial Services | Audit trail storage | SEC 17a-4, MiFID II |
| Healthcare | Medical record archives | HIPAA, GDPR |
| Legal | eDiscovery preservation | Legal hold requirements |
| Government | Records retention | NARA, agency mandates |

### Why This Market

1. **Determinism matters more than speed** — Regulators require reproducible archives
2. **Integrity is legally required** — Cryptographic verification has value
3. **Long-term compatibility is critical** — 7-25 year retention periods
4. **Premium pricing acceptable** — Compliance is a cost center, not profit center

## Product Tiers

### Tier 1: VECTRA Community (Free)
- VECTRA-Core reference implementation
- Full source code access
- No support, no SLA
- License: Evaluation/Research only

### Tier 2: VECTRA Professional ($12,000/year)
- Commercial license
- Binary distribution rights
- Format compatibility guarantee
- 90-day email support
- Annual version updates

### Tier 3: VECTRA Enterprise ($50,000/year)
- Everything in Professional
- 24/7 support SLA
- Custom integration assistance
- Certified builds (signed)
- Audit documentation package
- 10-year artifact compatibility guarantee

### Tier 4: VECTRA Compliance ($120,000/year)
- Everything in Enterprise
- Industry-specific certification assistance
- Regulatory documentation templates
- On-site compliance review
- White-glove migration support
- Named account manager
