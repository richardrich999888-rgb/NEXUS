## Core Principles

### 1. **Accountability by Architecture**
Every action crosses a commitment membrane. High-consequence actions require:
- Entropy expenditure (computational cost)
- Authority verification (delegation chains)
- External attestation (validator consensus)
- Trust accumulation (behavioral history)

### 2. **Bio-Inspired Regulation**
AI systems have "endocrine" states that:
- Decay over time (half-lives)
- Saturate under stress (receptor limits)
- Adapt to chronic conditions (allostasis)
- Follow circadian rhythms (time-aware)

### 3. **Immune Defense**
Autonomous systems that:
- Distinguish "self" from "non-self" behavior
- Detect coordinated defection (collusion)
- Quarantine rogue agents
- Build memory of past threats

### 4. **Unforkable Trust**
Trust that:
- Cannot be copied or transferred
- Is earned through consistent behavior
- Degrades when violated
- Survives across system restarts
