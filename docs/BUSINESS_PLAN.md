# AURA Protocol - Business Plan

## Executive Summary

AURA Protocol provides quantum-resistant, infrastructure-less verification that replaces trusted third parties (Visa, SWIFT, Certificate Authorities, DNS) with mathematical resonance. **Revenue from Day 1**.

## Revenue Model

### 1. SDK Licensing (Metered Billing)
- **Free tier**: 10 million verifications/month
- **Paid**: $0.001 per verification after limit
- **Volume discounts**: Up to 50% for 10M+ verifications

### 2. Runtime Fees
- **0.1 basis points** (0.001%) on value transferred
- Example: $1M transfer = $10 fee
- Applicable to payment/settlement use cases

### 3. Enterprise Licensing
- **$10,000/month** for unlimited verifications
- Priority support, SLAs
- Custom integrations

### 4. Patent Licensing
- **1% of revenue** for large-scale integrators
- Example: Visa integration = $50M+/year
- 10+ patent claims covering RIA fundamentals
