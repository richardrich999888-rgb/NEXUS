## Key §3(k) Drafting Strategy

To survive Section 3(k), every patent should be drafted as:

1. **"A system comprising..."** — not "A method implemented by software..."
2. **Emphasize physical effects:** Memory constraints, CPU limits, hardware interlocks
3. **Avoid:** "Algorithm," "program," "software"
4. **Use:** "Apparatus," "system," "engine," "controller," "gate"

## Month 8–10: PCT from India Priority

Use Indian provisionals as **priority date** for PCT filing. This gives:
- Indian patent protection (domestic)
- 30-month window to enter US, EU, Singapore national phases
- Priority date advantage over competitors

# HONEST CAVEATS FOR INDIA

1. **Section 3(k):** Indian law excludes "computer programme per se." Strong technical drafting is critical. ~30% risk of rejection for software-adjacent claims.
2. **Low commercialization:** Only 1.59% of Indian patents are commercialized. Licensing market is nascent.
3. **Weak enforcement:** Indian courts are slow; patent litigation takes 3–7 years. Damages awards are historically low.
4. **AI grant ratio:** Only 0.37% of AI patents filed in India are granted. Expect higher rejection rates.
5. **But:** India provides **priority date** for PCT → US filing. The India filing is strategically essential even if Indian commercial value is lower.
